Goal (roadmap R17): new things to wear for every growth stage and a celebration scene when he moves to a new stage
(friends, a cake, a gift, a photo for the album).

## Design
- 9 new clothes, one per stage 1…9 (`gift: <stage>`, `age: <stage>`): Чепчик (Малыш), Слюнявчик (Карапуз), Панамка (Детёныш),

  an open front over a white tee, keds as feet with a white toe/sole). Headphones keep his hair (not a hat).
  They are also in the wardrobe (locked by stage, tagged «Подарок на празднике») — an old save past the stage can buy them.
- Growth party = FRIENDS party kind 'grow' (startParty({ kind: 'grow', stage })): after the «подрос» card is closed (S.grow.party =
  pending stage, survives a reload; waits while he sleeps / a game / a sheet / another scene), the friends come in («Сюрприз! С новым
  этапом!»), a two-tier cake with a number candle (the stage), he blows it, a friend gives the stage gift (it lands on him: owned + worn;
  already owned → +30 coins), «Ух ты! Как раз по размеру!», cake bites, a countdown group photo → album 'grow:<stage>'
  «Праздник: <stage name>», bye. It doesn't use up the daily «Все вместе» (partyDay untouched).
- The quiet 'grow:N' moment shot of R16 goes away (the party photo replaces it).
